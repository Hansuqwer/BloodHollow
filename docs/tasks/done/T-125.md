# T-125 — T-118 follow-on: r18→r22 at the M3 gate — the reach stops being luck, and the "kiter" was a no-op  [DONE 2026-09-13]

## Context

T-118's r8→r17 series (devlog 0084, merged as `2a813ec`) closed with the hard
gate passed **once** — leg r9, 3/5 bots into the Drowned Crypt Depths at
692.9/692.5/617.1 s — and the boss verdict **not established**: no leg ever
engaged the Gravemother, and r14's restoration of r9's exact posture did not
reproduce the reach, so the entry was filed as luck-dependent. The director
picked the follow-on: restore r9's map-1 posture as **r18**, plus one untried
content-side fix from `docs/handover/T-118-r17.md` §5.

The series' DB and grind logs were gone with the previous sandbox, so the
staging had to be rebuilt before any leg could mean anything (see Deviations).

## Scope

- **r18** — restore r9's map-1 posture: individual return march (drop r13's
  map-1 column glue) and cap-cross at **q≥2** instead of r13's q≥3, keeping
  r16b's direction-aware election and guarded desync re-adoption.
- **r19** — handover §5 fix #2, untried until now: **distance-aligned marks**.
  The raider marks the **nearest** aggroed target (lowest-HP as tie-break)
  instead of lowest-HP, because distance is the one field a 3-tile-tight
  column agrees on while per-peer last-seen hp is stale.
- **r20** — a **bug found by the r19 forensics**, not a planned experiment:
  the Firebolt cast and the kiter band were gated on `kitClass == 3`
  (Cultist). `shared/content/kits.h` gives ch5 Firebolt to the **Gravecaller**
  (unlock L1) and leaves the Cultist's ch5 at **0**, so `World::trySkill`
  (`server/src/world.cpp:814-815`) dropped every such cast at the unlock gate.
  Both gates moved to `kitClass == 2`, and the channel table is now pinned.
- `tools/m3_topup.sh`: added the idempotent gear/stat seed the handover always
  claimed it had (Pit Blade `2002` + Hide Armor `2101` equipped,
  `str/vit/dex = 7/11/6`).
- `tests/test_kits.cpp`: six new assertions in the existing kit-dispatch case.

No server, shared, client or engine code changed. Journal epoch stays **21**,
schema **v11**, wire **237**.

1. **The reach is no longer luck-dependent** — the card's headline. Two
   consecutive legs reached the font (3/5, then 4/5) where the previous ten
   legs produced one reach. r19's 147 s entry is ~4× faster than r9's 617 s.
2. **r18 bought penetration, not the finish:** zero entries, but all five bots
   reached map-3 **x=38–39** (the barrow corridor) against r17's x=24.
3. **The "kiter" never existed.** The whole r8→r19 series fought melee-only;
   the Cultist's bolt was dropped server-side every 2.5 s. Fixing the gate
   took the reach from 3/5 to 4/5 and produced the first elite kills at this
   gate (`elites=1` on four bots).
4. **Boss verdict: still NOT established.** The party arrives on map 5 at
   84–100% hp and died crossing the ~25 tiles from the portal `(3,30)` to the
   font camp `(22,3)` — deepest x **12** — because map 5's route table had a
   single node, so the crossing was an unstaged dive. Finding 5 fixes that;
   the verdict is still open one layer deeper.
5. **r21 — a staged map-5 route turned the crossing into fights the party can win.**
   Four nodes on the verified walkable corridor, **(6,21) → (13,10) →
   (22,10) → (22,3)**, gave the stack staged fights: map-5 trace count
   **24 → 122**, elites **1 → 3–5**, trash 2–4 → 12–14, reach 4/5 at
   232–238 s (r20: 268–272 s).
   - Geometry was verified against the derived blocked grid rather than read
     from the handover: the handover's earlier r18–r20 form suggested nodes
     at **(8,20)** and **(14,12)** — both **BLOCKED**. Corrected in
     `docs/handover/T-125-r18-r22.md` §6 so it is not re-suggested.
   - **Where it stops now:** the party clears (6,21), holds (13,10), and dies
     at **x=15** inside **three simultaneous aggro fields** — apse elite
     (16,4) r8 hp 380, Sexton (21,5) r8 hp 420, Gravemother (21,2) r8 hp 700.
     Deepest x = 15 of 22; the last 7 tiles are the whole fight.
6. **r22 — the kiter band on map 5 was attempted, never exercised, and reverted.**
   The Gravemother's bolt range is 6 and our Firebolt's is 8, so the
   Gravecaller can trade from outside her reach — if it stops walking into a
   40-dmg slam. Two legs ran that binary and **neither reached the code**:
   r22 never entered map 5 (618 of 791 traces on map 1, deepest map-3 x=36);
   r22b put two bots on map 5 for **1.3 s and 2.3 s**, stuck at node (6,21),
   16 traces total. The change is provably inert on maps 1 and 3 — where both
   legs were lost — so the gap from r21 is **leg variance, not the lever**.
   Reverted rather than left in: the band ends in `continue`, so an
   unexercised map-5 branch is exactly the **r8c** stall risk sitting inside
   the best-known configuration. Re-land it only behind a leg that reaches
   the font, and keep the front-runner guard.
   - **Leg variance is large.** Same binary, same staging: r22 → 0/5,
     r22b → 2/5, r21 → 4/5. **One leg cannot retire a lever, and one cannot
     promote one either.**

## Deviations

- **Staging rebuilt and disclosed.** The series' DB was gone. Five fresh
  accounts were registered, then `m3_topup.sh` staged L13 / 7-11-6 /
  Pit Blade + Hide Armor / 16 vials / map 3 `(1,22)` with `class_id` seeded
  (the in-game oath still rejects L13). Absolute death counts are therefore
  comparable in kind to r9–r17, not a continuation of the same characters.

  claimed. Without it the top-up force-sets level 13 on a character still
  holding creation fists, which would have made every follow-on leg
  uninterpretable.
- **r20 was not a planned experiment.** It is a defect fix found in the r19
  forensics; it is reported separately from the two named experiments so the
  attribution stays clean.
