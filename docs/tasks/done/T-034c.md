# T-034c — L4-L5 gear-band investigation (gear-churn economics, not mob numbers) [DONE 2026-09-06]

## Acceptance criteria

Explain — from the duel table + the M2b-final rerun — *why* the campaign
plateaus at L4-L5 even after T-034b's gnoll/widow retune, and name the
concrete lever(s) that move it. Deliver a quantitative model of the L4-L5
gear-churn (gold in, gear cost, XP in, death tax out) plus a ranked lever
shortlist. No mob-damage nudge: the wall is gear, not numbers.

## Findings

### 1. The plateau is a death-tax treadmill

The rerun legs show 114–141 kills and **24–48 deaths per 9-minute leg**
(2 bots). The death tax (`world.cpp killPlayer`) is `10 + (L-1)·15/24`% of
the current bar, with `refBar = xpNext(level)`:

| level | xpNext | tax % | XP lost per death |
|---|---|---|---|
| L4 | 1300 | 11% | 143 |
| L5 | 1960 | 12% | 235 |
| L6 | 2750 | 13% | 357 |

Gross kill XP ≈ 114–141 kills × ~90–110 XP (ghoul spine) ≈ **11–14k XP/leg**.
Death debt ≈ ~40 deaths × ~190 XP ≈ **7.6k XP/leg**. The two are the same
order of magnitude, so at L5 the treadmill is near break-even — which is
exactly the observed plateau (legs end L4 or L5, never climb past).

### 2. The wall is gear, located in the hound band (duel table s22)

At player **L4** vs Hollow Hound (L5: 100 hp / 13 dmg / 7 def):

| loadout | wins | end-hp fraction |
|---|---|---|
| fists | 1/9 | 0.00 |
| Rusty Shank (80g) | 4/9 | 0.00 |
| Pit Blade (260g) | 7/9 | **0.30** |
| blade + Hide Armor (120g) | 9/9 | 0.46 |

Blade-only wins the hound 7/9 but ends at **30% hp**; the hound marsh is a
pack camp (leash-16, 8 maxAlive), so the second hound kills a 30%-hp bot.
Hide Armor (def 6) is what flips 7/9 → 9/9 *and* lifts end-hp to a
survivable 0.46. At L5 the blade alone is 9/9 — the band is exactly where
the bot must buy armor but reliably hasn't yet.

### 3. Gear is slow because shopping is coupled to death

To be hound-capable the bot needs **380g of gear** (Pit Blade 260 + Hide
Armor 120) against **50g start**. Gold in the low band is ~16–22g/kill net
(rats 6–14g + pelt 4g; ghouls 14–30g + finger 8.8g), so the blade is ~12–15
clean kills and armor ~6–8 more — affordable in a couple of minutes *of
uninterrupted farming*.

But the campaign profile has **no proactive re-gear trip**. Every shop
branch is gated on `nearTown` (≤4 tiles of the bindstone), and the only
home-bound `InputPath` is the pilgrim anvil trip. A campaigner reaches
`nearTown` only at leg start and on **death-respawn**. So gear acquisition
is hitched to the death cycle: the bot spends real time in the
blade-only-but-no-armor window, dying to the second hound, respawning at
town, shopping, walking back out, dying again.

### 4. Secondary: the L3+ camp sits in the over-level triangle

The ghoul-spine camp (55,19) neighbours ghouls_east (52,17),
ghouls_orchard (44,26), gnolls_pits (50,42) and widow_glade (58,42). The
"no walls" filter only skips `level > own+2` mobs when they are `d > 2`;
an adjacent gnoll (L7) or widow (L9) is still pulled, and both kill a
blade-only L4 bot. The cross-zone excursion at L5 adds the mine/crypt
spawner packs to the same exposure.

## Lever shortlist (ranked by impact × safety)

| # | Lever | Change | Impact | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Campaign re-gear trip | Add "walk home when gold ≥ next gear tier" (blade @260g, then armor @120g) to the campaign profile | Decouples gear from death; breaks the treadmill | Bot-only; zero balance risk; replay-invariant (client-predicted path, server validates) |
| 2 | Gear affordability | Hide Armor 120→80 (match Rusty Shank) or Pit Blade 260→220 | Shrinks the under-geared window | Content/economy; value-table ripple |
| 3 | Hound-band number | Hound dmg 13→12 (blade-only L4 → 9/9) | Removes the gear-gate tutorial's sting | Reopens T-034b's "hound untouched" decision |
| 4 | Death-tax relief | Soften L4-L5 tax 11–12% → ~8% | Directly cuts the treadmill | Weakens the GDD "stakes" pillar |

**Recommendation:** lever 1 first — it is the actual bug (a human would not
grind to 48 deaths without re-gearing), it is the cheapest to verify
(replay must stay bit-exact; no content numbers move), and it directly
targets the mechanism the evidence points at. Measure; if lever 1 alone
does not clear L6, add lever 2. Levers 3–4 are content-pillar calls for the
director.
