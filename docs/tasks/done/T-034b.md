# T-034b — balance retune from the duel table (S22) [DONE 2026-09-06]

## Evidence basis

`logs/duel-table-s21.csv` + the M2b-final gate log: chain bots plateaued at
**L4-L6** across legs 5-12 (TARGET L8 NOT reached). The table shows exactly
where the wall stands:

- **Hollow Hound (L5)**: bare 1-5/9 at player L4; `blade+armor` = 9/9. The
  hound is the gear-gate tutorial and already works — untouched.
- **Bonepicker Gnoll (L7)**: bare **0/9** at player L6/7/9; even `blade`
  alone is 5-6/9. First hard wall on the ghoul spine.
- **Charnel Widow (L9)**: bare **0/9** at player L8/9/11; `blade+armor` 
  without vials is 1-3/9. The cocoon-side widow ends every bare mid-kit
  campaigner that wanders into the hatch.

## Lever shortlist (applied)

| Lever | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Charnel Widow dmg | 24 → **22** | 0/9 bare mid-kit; -2 per swing buys roughly one extra exchange per potion band |
| Charnel Widow leash | 12 → **10** | shorter de-aggro makes kite-to-leash-break a real tactic (leash break = walk home + full heal on the widow's side, bot disengages clean) |
| Bonepicker Gnoll dmg | 18 → **17** | 0/9 bare; -1 per swing moves the bare-blade coinflip at L6/7 |

What deliberately did **not** change: hp/def/xp/parts/gold (economy
invariants — anvil tolls and bounty payouts key off them), and the hound
entirely. The widow stays a gear-gate boss in solo duels (bare rows remain
0/9 in `duel-table-s22.csv` — by design); the levers target the campaign
pack context, where the leash break + party play (S13) + choir support
(T-054b) tip the fight.
