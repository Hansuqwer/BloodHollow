# T-113 — L9 pace logistics: scoping card (DESIGN, no code)  [DONE 2026-09-12]

## Context

Board row after T-100: the L8→L9 ladder is open (n=2 pairs, ~80% of the bar
per 540 s leg, zero widow kills suffered, zero L11 contact) and the binding
constraint is **logistics** — vial flow + bridge travel (~30 town trips per
leg) — not mob stats. T-095's tuning gate stays shut. This card scopes the
logistics levers into one concrete, minimal-risk implementation card, per
devlog 0059's "a forward operating base would move the pace more than any
mob number".

## Findings (from T-090/T-100 telemetry + code law; no new legs run)

1. **The server already permits vial stockpiling.** Marta's stock
   (`content::kVendorStock`) is a static line-item list with **no per-item
   depletion and no restock cadence**; `BuyRequest` accepts qty up to
   `stackMax` (Blood Vial 3001: stack 16, 30 g, heals 40). Nothing
   server-side caps belt depth.
2. **The 30-trip tax is bot policy, not economy law.**
   `tools/bots/main.cpp` campaign profile keeps a **4-deep belt**
   ("keep a 3-deep flask belt for packs") and buys **qty = 2** per town
   visit when gold ≥ 90. With the pair drinking 37–40 vials/leg, an 8-vial
   pair capacity refilled 2-at-a-time mathematically produces the observed
   24–30 shop stops (plus pawn/rite stops).
3. **Gold income sustains deep belts by mid-leg.** Widow band 90–160 g,
   gnoll 60–110 g, ~150 kills/leg → ~1 000–4 000 g income vs ~1 200 g for
   40 vials. Only the early leg is gold-thin (start gold 50).
4. **Armor is a survival-margin lever, not a pace lever.** Damage law
   `raw·100/(100+DEF)`: Bone Plate (def 11) vs Hide (def 6) against widow
   raw 22 ≈ 19.8 vs 20.8 — about **1 hp/hit**; × 270 hits/leg ≈ 250–500 hp
   ≈ 6–12 vials (180–360 g), i.e. the 350 g plate amortizes over 1–2 legs.
   It buys crossing margin; it does not move the ~6 xp/s grind rate.
5. **Travel is the residual tax after belts are fixed.** Town (32,16) ↔
   camp (60,41) ≈ 40 tiles each way across the bridge gauntlet; the only
   deaths in two legs were L5 hounds on that road (2 then 0). A risk line,
   not a wall.

then re-run the T-090/T-100 discipline: same template DB (`/tmp/t084.db`
lineage), campaign pair sqlite-set L8/xp0/town (32,16), campaign-only
