# T-054 Cultist kit v1 (Pale Choir) — DONE S14 (2026-09-05)

## Context

Cultist-first per the Phase 3 queue. GDD Cultist list: Mend, Mass Mend, Bless,
Ironskin, Haste, Purify, Curse of Weakness, Raise Skeleton, Sanctuary,
Resurrect(20). v1 ships the party-survivability triangle — **Mend / Bless /
Ironskin** — plus the Gravecaller starter (Firebolt) so the dispatch table is
proven north-to-south (attack skill, heal, buff, nuke) rather than only with
support verbs.

## Scope

- **Mend** (channel 2, L1+): instant heal on self or party member within 6
  tiles, same zone, alive. Heal = 30 + 4·level; cost 8 MP; CD 25 t.
- **Bless** (channel 3, L3+): target self/party member; +10% hit & damage for
  6 000 t (5 min); stamp `blessUntil`; cost 15 MP; CD 40 t.
- **Ironskin** (channel 4, L6+): same targeting; +20% effective DEF 6 000 t;
  stamp `ironskinUntil`; cost 15 MP; CD 40 t.
- **Firebolt** (channel 5, Gravecaller L1+): ranged nuke, 8 tiles, dmg
  8 + 2·level unmitigated-by-armor magic (era: plague-fire ignores plate,
  balanced by cost), mob/player targets (PvP scalar 0.65 applies); 6 MP;
  CD 30 t.
- Combat hooks: `blessUntil` in the hit & damage paths (regular autoattack +
  Power Swing); `ironskinUntil` in mitigation (`def × 1.2`).
- Feedback: heal floaters (event kind reuse/extend), ch-2 choir lines
  ("X mends Y for N.", "the Choir blesses Y."), buff seconds visible in the
  stat panel; target-click on the party frame selects the recipient.
- Chorus (party-wide buffs L15/20), Haste, Purify, pet, Sanctuary, Resurrect:
  **out of scope v1**, listed so the dispatch design leaves room.

## Acceptance criteria

- A partied Cultist meaningfully reduces party deaths vs the S13 two-fighter
  leg at the same camps (measured in the S14 gate leg, T-055).
- Level gates enforced (L1 Mend, L3 Bless, L6 Ironskin); MP and CD enforced;
  heals never target mobs/enemies; buffs stack-free (re-cast refreshes).
- Replay bit-exact with buff stamps live.

## Tests required

Mend rules (self/party/range/mob-reject/MP/CD/level gate), exact heal number,
Bless hit+dmg modifier on/off expiry, Ironskin mitigation factor, Firebolt
range/dmg/PvP scalar, buff refresh semantics, replay-parity of a buffed fight.
