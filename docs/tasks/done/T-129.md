# T-129 — Blood Moon flag + two levers (H4 night war, 1/3)

## Context

MVP demands Blood Moon (GDD §9, MVP §1: day/night row). No moon state
exists; curse (T-070) and night bite (T-061) are the two night-stakes
levers to amplify. Shape follows the H1 siege-stub pattern (session
flags + journaled command + GM chat hook + quiet-fail repeats),
re-implemented here — H1 lives in PR #29's lineage, not this stack.

## Scope

- `World::bloodMoon`: session-scoped flag, `gm blood-moon` → journaled
  `Command::kBloodMoon` (open to all callers like H1's rehearsal hook; GM
  gating waits on accounts). One moon at a time + dead callers fail
  quiet. Active until next 05:00 dawn (`ticksUntilDawn` via `sim::hourAt`;
  exactly-at-dawn yields a full-day moon — documented, harmless).
  Fiction on chatCh 2 + `[moon]` printf. Never persisted; replay rebuilds
  it from the journaled command.
- Lever 1 — curse lasts 60 s under the moon: `curseDuration()` (2×
  `kCurseTicks` when active) at both application sites (slam + bolt).
- Lever 2 — night bite ×1.15 → ×1.30 under the moon: `nightBiteNum()`
  at the T-061 site.
- Scheduler hook for the weekly moon is Phase S business — this card
  ships the GM trigger + flag + plumbing and says so.
- NO epoch bump (flag defaults off; old journals never contain kBloodMoon
  → old replays bit-identical; proven by re-replaying `t128.bwj` mm=0).
  NO wire change (red tint is a client follow-up, filed).

## Acceptance criteria

- `gm blood-moon` raises the moon to next dawn; second call/dead caller
  silent; expiry automatic by tick; curse 600↔1200, bite 115↔130 follow
  the flag; paired-worlds hp-loss ratio ≈130/115 end-to-end.

## Tests required

- `tests/test_blood_moon.cpp`: activate/fiction/repeat/dead/expiry,
  `curseDuration` + `nightBiteNum` both states, journaled-path
  (`applyWorldCommand` kBloodMoon), paired-worlds fighter-loop ratio pin.

## Out of scope

- EK ledger + L19 oath (T-130) · siege scheduler (Phase S) · client red
  tint · GM auth gating · wire 237 untouched.
