# T-126 — Affix v2: table 3 → 10 with effect hooks (H3 loot depth, 1/5)

## Context

Affixes v1 (T-059) ships 3 drop-time one-liners (Whet/Warding/Leech), one per
item, rolled `rng_.range(1, kAffixCount)` at gear-drop time in `killMob`.
MVP loot needs a ~40-affix horizon with ~12 boss uniques; this card lays the
v2 foundation (7 new affixes, all effect-hooked) so the four boss-unique
cards (T-127..T-130) can reference fixed affix combos. The 10 → 40 table is a
filed follow-up, not this card.

## Scope

- `shared/content/items.h`: `kAffixNames` +4..10, `kAffixCount` 3 → 10.
  - 4 of the Ox (armor): +20 hpMax while equipped & awake.
  - 5 of Thorns (armor): reflect 2 to the attacker per landed swing,
    never lands the kill (floor at 1). Swings only, not bolts.
  - 6 of Focus (weapon): +4 ACC (≈+4% hit, GDD §4 scale).
  - 7 of Embers (weapon): +2 flat dmg, +1 more at night.
  - 8 of the Vigil (any equipped gear): +2 light radius while a torch burns
    or the lantern is raised (name continuity with T-ART-03).
  - 9 of Greed (weapon): +10% kill gold (multiplies before the chaotic
    moral 115%).
  - 10 of Mending (armor): +1 HP per OOC regen proc.
- `server/src/world.cpp`: `hasAffix` probe (equipped + slot-gated + awake);
  hooks in `recomputeHpMax`, `resolveHit` (thorns), `effAcc`,
  `equippedWeaponDmg`, torch/lantern set sites, kill-gold site, OOC regen;
  `toggleEquip` re-syncs hpMax on armor toggles only (Ox materializes on
  armor equip/unequip, not just level-up; weapon toggles never touched the
  cap). Known edge, flagged for follow-up: Ox armor going dormant mid-fight
  keeps the +20 until the next recompute trigger.
- Mismatched slot (e.g. Ox on a weapon) = flavor text only (v1 precedent:
  Whet on armor is mute).
- `kJournalEpoch` 21 → 22: the roll widens 1..3 → 1..10, shifting every
  downstream draw in journals containing gear drops. Fresh gate leg
  `logs/t126.bwj` ships with this card.

## Acceptance criteria

- All 7 effects observable in-game; dormant (0-durability) gear mutes all.
- One-per-item invariant holds; affix u8 round-trips the 7-field blob.
- Full battery green (§Tests) + `t126.bwj` replay `mismatches=0` + epoch
  guard refuses `t120.bwj` (21 vs 22) with exit 4.
