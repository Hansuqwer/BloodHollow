## Verdict (T-113 criteria)

- ✅ vial-driven town stops ≤ 8/leg — **8 and 5** (2/1 of those were regear;
  vial stops 6/4). The scoped mechanism works: 24–30 → 5–8, a 3–5× cut.
- ❌ bar ≥ 90%/leg **or** L9 inside 540 s — 73.5%/55% avg (n=2 spread
  brackets T-090, never reaches T-100). **Pace hypothesis REFUTED.**
- ✅ deaths ≤ 2 — 0 and 0, killer histograms empty (no L9/L11, no Maw).
- ✅ replay mismatches=0 — bot-only, epoch stays 18.

**The trip tax was real but it was not the pace.** With stops collapsed
3–5×, the bar did not move faster: kills plateau at 123–157 (historical
band 149–156) while engagement heat ROSE (hurt 298/472 vs 270/270, mend
35/53) — continuous camp presence trades the retreat-home healing rotation
for standing trades, and the camp's respawn + TTK throughput is the
ceiling either way. Logistics does not own L9 pace; the camp does.
