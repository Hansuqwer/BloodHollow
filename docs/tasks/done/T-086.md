- **Pelts bind first:** rats drop pelts at 40%, bats at 25%, ghouls drop none (4002). Pilgrims level out of the rat camp in ~30–100 s; expected leg income ≈ 10–15 rat kills × 40% ≈ **4–6 pelts vs 30 needed** — 5–6× short on the richest leg ever. `dbgReady` is 0 in every leg: bots are never even "ready but not in reach" (`dbgNoAnvil` also 0).
- **Skill binds second:** `tryAnvil` refuses below sword skill 20 (`world.cpp:1498`); L2–L4 pilgrims in a 240–540 s leg never approach it.
- **Gold binds third (soft):** 120g is reachable (mGold up to 442), and the anvil sits at the town spawn plaza (`spawnAnvils`, zone 1) so proximity is reachable — neither is the blocker.
- **Blade time dominates:** `dbgNoBlade` ≈ 2100/leg — pilgrims spend most samples pre-equipped (50g start vs 110g shank), so the graft pipeline never even starts most legs.
