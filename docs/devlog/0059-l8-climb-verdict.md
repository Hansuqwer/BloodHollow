# 0059 — L9 is a walk, not a wall (T-090)

T-090, bot-only. The question was whether the widow camp is a second barricade. It is not — it is a long walk with hot weather.

## The leg in one line

156 kills, 2 roam deaths, 76% + 57% of the 4690 bar, zero widow kills suffered, zero L11 contact, replay clean.

## What that means

- **No wall:** nothing at the camp kills L8 pairs. Both deaths were L5 hounds at (21,27) — bridge-gauntlet country on a vial run, the pre-existing toll road, not the new content.
- **The real tax is logistics:** 30 shop trips in 540 s (a town run every ~18 s for the pair, mostly vials: 37 drunk). Town (32,16) to camp (60,41) is ~40 tiles each way across the bridge gauntlet. Every crossing risks hounds; every minute in town is 0 xp/s. A forward operating base (vial stockpiling? armor upgrade?) would move the pace more than any mob number.
- **The camp is hot but fair:** 270 hits taken vs 217 swings — the pair absorbs more than it deals and converts potions + mends (36) into XP. Widow dmg 22 vs 136 hp + armor is exactly the "punitive but survivable" band the GDD wants above the gnolls.
- **#6 (widow/gnoll tuning) stays parked:** there is no wall to tune. If the director wants faster L9, the levers are logistics (vial economy, armor, route), not mob stats — and even that waits for a repeat leg, because n=1 pair is a anecdote with a replay log, not a measurement.

## Judgment calls

- Started the bar at xp 0 (not the banked 2112) to measure the full climb — a harder test than resuming, chosen on purpose.
- Did not extend to 750 s for the ding: the pace extrapolates cleanly and a second 540 s leg (repeat discipline) is the honest next step, not a longer first leg.
