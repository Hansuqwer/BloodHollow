## 2. What actually moved the needle

1. **Distance-aligned marks (r19).** Five bots inside 3 tiles of each other
   were each marking their own "lowest-HP" target from their own stale
   last-seen-hp view — five rankings, DPS split five ways. Nearest-first
   converges by construction. This is what brought the reach back, and it
   brought it back **4× faster** than r9 (147 s vs 617 s).
2. **A working Firebolt (r20).** 3/5 → 4/5 and the first elite kills.
3. **A staged map-5 route (r21).** Map 5 had ONE node at (22,3), so the
   crossing from the portal (3,30) was a single 46-step dive; r19/r20 died
   inside it at a deepest x of 12. Four nodes on the verified walkable
   corridor — **(6,21) → (13,10) → (22,10) → (22,3)** — turned it into
   staged fights the stack could win: map-5 trace count went 24 → **122**,
   and the party killed 3–5 elites each.

## 3. r22: attempted, never exercised, reverted

The plan was to switch the kiter band on at the font so the Gravecaller
trades bolts from 7–8 (the Gravemother's bolt range is 6, our Firebolt's is
8) instead of walking into a 40-dmg slam.

Two legs ran that binary and **neither reached the code**:

- **r22** never entered map 5 — 618 of 791 traces were on map 1, deepest
  map-3 x=36, node (39,12) visited 5 times against r21's 115.
- **r22b** put two bots on map 5 for **1.3 s and 2.3 s**, stuck at node
  (6,21), 16 traces total. The band never fired at the font.

The change is provably inert on maps 1 and 3 (`bandMap` is false there), and
that is where both legs were lost — so the difference from r21 is leg
variance, not the lever. Reverted rather than left in: the band ends in
`continue`, so an unexercised map-5 branch is precisely the r8c stall risk
sitting inside the best-known configuration. **Re-land it only behind a leg
that reaches the font**, and keep the front-runner guard (never band the
runner — its route machine is the column's).

## 7. Environment (carry-over, plus one new landmine)

- `export PATH=$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH` every call; `ninja -C build/verify`.
- **A sandbox restart wipes `.local/` and `build/`** (both are excluded from
  snapshots): cmake, ninja, the whole build tree and `build/m3_gate.db`
  vanish, and **`.git` can roll back to an older snapshot while the working
  tree keeps newer content** — HEAD appeared at `818d661` with committed work
  showing as uncommitted diffs. Recovery: `git ls-remote` to confirm the
  pushed sha still exists, `git fetch origin <branch>`, then
  `git reset --mixed <sha>` (moves the ref and index, leaves the tree). Then
  re-install cmake/ninja and rebuild. **Push early, push often.**
- `ninja -C build/verify bh_maps` before any test/replay run, else four
  `loadZone` tests fail and replay cannot open the maps.
- Replay is `bh_server --replay-world <journal>`; there is no `bh_replay`.
- No cmake/ninja on a fresh sandbox: `pip install --user
  --break-system-packages cmake ninja`. apt is blocked → headless battery
  (3 client-law TUs go to CI).
