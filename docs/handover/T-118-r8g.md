The route node **(22,21)** sits **inside the leash of BOTH packs**: the racks
(leash **14**, node is 6–7 tiles from the rack edge) and the cocoon (leash
**10**, 4–5 tiles from the cocoon edge). A fight there means both packs are
permanently in leash — swarm 8–10 reforms every 30 s. In every full leg the
column arrives as a healthy stack, the pile fight lasts 30–60 s, and **2–3 of
5 die** (r8e4: 3 dead; r8f: 2 dead; r8g: 3 dead, then the remaining 2 died as
well → the §0 all-five-in-town loop). The column then loses the numbers it
needs for the barrow ring (~1900 hp, must be met 5-deep) and the font.

**Candidate fixes, smallest first (none tried yet — pick one, or design
better):**

1. **Drop the quorum wait at the node — the r4-style unbroken pass.** No
   quorum at (22,21) (quorum 0 / cap 0); the runner advances immediately on
   arrival; millers hold only point-blank. The column crosses the
   double-leash rect in ~10–15 s, before the pile can form; the 30 s respawn
   can't keep pace. Smallest diff; matches the original r4 design intent.
   Risk: the pile still forms if the stack is slow.
2. **Align focus-fire marks by distance.** All five currently mark
   "lowest-HP aggroed" from their own (stale, per-peer) views → marks
   disagree → DPS dilutes 2–3×. Mark **nearest aggroed target** instead:
   bots at the same stack position converge on the same target. Small diff;
   compounds fix 1 (the r8 hand of the pair).
3. **Move the node** off the double-leash overlap (e.g. (28,21): east of the
   racks, still west of the Cantor's aggro) so the pile forms in
   single-aggro territory. Verify any new node against both aggro-6 rects
   and the leash-14 circle before committing.
4. **Kill the pile before the fight:** the runner pre-marks and the stack
   kites the racks edge-on so ghouls engage one-at-a-time instead of
   swarming the node.
5. **Accept the attrition:** the party arrives at the barrow 3-deep and the
   verdict is "the gauntlet attrition is the wall, not the boss." A valid
   finding ONLY IF the font is still reached — the 3 survivors must then beat
   the ring (~1900 hp at ~70 dps ≈ 27 s — marginal).

Also open (lower priority): miller ±1–2 tile oscillation (the catch-up path

**Trace-field guide** — `[trace]` lines every 5 s in `logs/m3_gate_bots.log`:


(100 = none) · `swarm` = aggressive mobs in range · **TEMP debug fields**
