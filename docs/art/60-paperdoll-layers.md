**MVP composite rule (bible §9 "tint/attach"):** layers 1–4 are **baked** per
(class, sex) sheet at B6; layers 5–6 are runtime attachments only if the
client grows an attachment pass — otherwise the class-iconic weapon is baked
too and item weapons show only in the icon/inventory until later. This
keeps B6 to 6 sheets (3 classes × m/f) ± tones.

## Cells (both drafted — D2)

| | D2a 32×48 | D2b 32×56 |
|---|---|---|
| body height (S idle) | 46 px | 52 px |
| feet row (anchorY) | 42 | 50 |

So the real D2a body is **43–44 px**, not 46; the style-tile README's "46 px body" is corrected to 43. Registry amended. | 52 px = rows 0..50 (head row 0, no clip) if `fit_to_cell(51)`; use 50 to keep 1 px headroom |
| head size | 8 px | 9 px |
| shoulder width Rav/Cul/Gc | 14 / 11 / 10 px | 16 / 12 / 11 px |
| weapon tip may exit cell | top by ≤ 2 px (allowed) | ≤ 2 px |
| name tag y (game.cpp) | −52 (10 px above cell top) | −52 becomes **inside** the cell top (cell top = −50); tag needs −60 → engine constant change, or accept overlap. **Flag for D2.** |
| HP bar y | −46 | −46 sits 4 px below cell top — same issue |

So D2b (32×56) has a hidden cost beyond `anchorY`: the two overhead offsets
(`game.cpp:750–751` name at y−52, HP bar at y−46 per `drawRemoteEnt`) are
tuned for a 48-cell and would collide with a 56-cell head. Two more constants
in T-ART-10 if 56 is chosen.

## Pivot table (right hand, per dir) — S-idle, D2a cell; D2b = same +8 px in y

| dir | row | hand (x,y) | weapon angle | facing note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | 0 | (24, 28) | 20° | blade toward +x, hilt visible |
| SE | 1 | (23, 30) | 35° | blade in front of hip |
| S | 2 | (21, 31) | 45° | blade crosses the front (cleaver held low, style tile) |
| SW | 3 | (14, 31) | 60° | hand still *right* → appears on screen-left of the body: blade overlaps torso — draw weapon **behind** torso layer for SW/W/NW |
| W | 4 | (9, 28) | 90° | weapon behind body; only tip and hilt show |
| NW | 5 | (10, 26) | 120° | behind |
| N | 6 | (13, 25) | 150° | behind, tip above shoulder |
| NE | 7 | (20, 26) | 165° | in front again |

Rule: the weapon draws **in front** for dirs E/SE/S/NE and **behind** for
SW/W/NW/N — this is why the right-hand rule cannot be satisfied by mirroring.
Pixel positions are the *proposed* pivots for the base body; B6 measures the
real ones from the S/SE/E plates and fills the JSON below.

## Skin tone index map

Family strips reserve the **last 4 indices** for skin (see
`assets/aigen/palettes/families.json` → `skin_slots`). Tone swap =
replace those 4 entries: pale `#6a5252 → #d6c6be`, sallow `#5a4838 → #b8a48a`,
weathered `#402e24 → #8e6e52`. If no shader (D7): bake three sheets per
(class, sex) = 18 sheets, ~33 KB each (D2a) — acceptable.
